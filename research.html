Honors Undergraduate Thesis · University of Central Florida

When Speech and Language Diverge

Racial and Ethnic Patterns in Florida Speech and Language Impairment Program Membership, 2017–2025

Question

What becomes visible when Florida’s Speech Impairment and Language Impairment classifications are analyzed separately rather than collapsed into the combined federal S/LI category?

Design

Repeated cross-sectional administrative study spanning 67 county school districts and eight school years. The analysis compares Black and Hispanic students with White students using rates, risk ratios, district patterns, decomposition, and prespecified sensitivity analyses.

Data
Florida Department of Education administrative workbooks

Scale
16 workbooks · 1,608 district-year-race rows

Methods
R · administrative data · risk ratios · panel description · sensitivity analysis

Status
Analysis frozen and reproducible; manuscript in progress

Interpretive boundary: program-membership classifications are administrative outcomes. They are not direct measures of disorder prevalence, diagnostic accuracy, individual substitution between categories, discrimination, or actual service receipt.

Research Assistant · University of California, Irvine · PI: Andrew Penner

California School Lunch & Attendance Project

Problem

Public California data provide TK/K absence days but do not publish the corresponding student-specific expected attendance days required to directly identify annual student-days attended.

Approach

I build and maintain a reproducible R pipeline integrating state enrollment, absenteeism, and school data; evaluate alternative denominator constructions; validate measures against Irvine and Lodi district records; and document where the public data stop supporting stronger claims.

Data
California Department of Education + district records

Methods
R · data integration · validation · measurement · sensitivity analysis

Principle
Do not relabel assumption-dependent proxies as observed quantities

Independent research

Who Gets Help First?

Racial and Socioeconomic Differences in the Timing of Developmental Service Entry Among U.S. Children

Using pooled 2023–2024 National Survey of Children’s Health data, I examine racial/ethnic and socioeconomic differences in developmental-service initiation among children who received developmental special services. The workflow uses complex-survey weighting, multiply imputed household poverty data, multivariable models, marginal probabilities, and robustness analyses.

Policy companion

What Federal Special-Education Data Hide

A Policy Case for Disaggregating Speech and Language Impairment

This project studies the reporting architecture surrounding the federal Speech or Language Impairment category. It pairs a source-exhaustion audit with a proposed layered reporting standard that begins with native state values, records crosswalk rules and mapping confidence, and permits an explicit “unable to map without inference” disposition.
