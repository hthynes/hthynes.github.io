Research approach

I treat administrative disability categories as records of institutional decisions, not as transparent measures of disabled people.

Approach

Institutions do more than record disability.

Schools identify, classify, place, support, and discipline students through rules and practices shaped by particular histories and ideas about disability, race, language, behavior, and need.

My current quantitative work examines patterns in those institutional records. I am also interested in historical and community-engaged research that can address questions administrative data cannot answer.

Federal reportingSpeech or Language ImpairmentSLI
Florida reports Speech Impairment and Language Impairment separately. Federal special-education reporting generally combines them.

My honors thesis uses Florida Department of Education data from 2017–2025 to examine racial and ethnic patterns in SI and LI program membership across all 67 county school districts.
