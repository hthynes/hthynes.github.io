When Speech and Language Diverge

What changes when Speech Impairment and Language Impairment are analyzed separately?

Using eight years of Florida Department of Education records, I built and validated a county-level dataset spanning all 67 Florida county school districts. The study examines racial and ethnic patterns while preserving Florida’s separate SI and LI classifications.

04

About

I started college planning to become a school-based speech-language pathologist.

As I learned more about special education, I became more interested in the decisions schools make around disability and services: why some children enter special education and others don’t, why racial patterns differ across disability categories, and how school resources and state definitions can change what happens to a child.

Those questions brought me to sociology. Speech and language stayed with me, and they now sit at the center of my honors thesis.
