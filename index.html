Sociology of race, disability & institutionsOrlando · 2026

I study race, disability, and institutions.

I study how institutions define and respond to human difference, and how racism, ableism, white supremacy, and socioeconomic inequality shape the lives and opportunities of Black disabled children and youth from low-resource communities.

Research approach

I treat administrative disability categories as records of institutional decisions, not as transparent measures of disabled people.

Approach

Institutions help make difference consequential.

Schools and other institutions classify, support, discipline, and allocate resources through rules and practices shaped by histories of race, disability, language, gender, and class.

I study those processes through quantitative research while building toward historical, archival, and community-engaged work on how categories, professional knowledge, and institutional authority are made and contested.

Speech and language impairment in Florida schools

Federal reportingSpeech or Language ImpairmentSLI
Florida reports Speech Impairment and Language Impairment separately. Federal special-education reporting generally combines them.

My honors thesis uses Florida Department of Education data from 2017–2025 to examine racial and ethnic patterns in SI and LI program membership across all 67 county school districts.

Questions I’m studying

Four questions across my current work

01 · Classification

How do racial and ethnic patterns in special education change when Speech Impairment and Language Impairment are analyzed separately?

02 · Service timing

Among children who receive developmental services, how does the timing of entry differ by race, ethnicity, and household poverty?

03 · Federal reporting

How does combining Speech Impairment and Language Impairment in federal reporting obscure racial and ethnic differences between the two classifications?

04 · Policy change

Did state Medicaid “free care” expansions change the share of school-age children identified for special education?
