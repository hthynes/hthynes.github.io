Sociology · education · disability

I study how schools decide who gets identified for support and who receives it.

I’m a sociology researcher at the University of Central Florida. My work centers Black and Hispanic/Latino children from low-resource communities and asks how special education and developmental services are identified, provided, and recorded in data.

Featured research

When Speech and Language Diverge

My honors thesis uses eight years of Florida Department of Education records across all 67 county school districts to examine Speech Impairment and Language Impairment separately.

Florida keeps these two classifications separate while federal reporting combines them. I built the dataset from 16 state workbooks and test whether racial and ethnic patterns change when the distinction is preserved.
