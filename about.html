About

Taiel Lucile

I’m an undergraduate sociology researcher at the University of Central Florida studying education, disability, organizations, and social stratification.

I’m especially interested in moments when institutions turn human differences into administrative categories: who gets identified, how categories are defined, what information disappears when categories are aggregated, and how those decisions shape access to resources.

That interest has taken me across special-education administrative data, national child-health surveys, school attendance and meal data, Medicaid policy, municipal childcare systems, community-engaged education research, and earlier laboratory work on aging and hearing.

I want my scholarship to remain accountable to the communities represented in the data while being rigorous about the limits of what those data can tell us.
